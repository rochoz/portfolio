## Design Specificity Verdict

The service mix and candid scope notes are specific to Santiago’s freelance offer. The olive-and-amber language remains coherent with the portfolio. The new feature-plus-groups composition differentiates the entry package from other work; the structure is less interchangeable than the previous six-card grid. The copy still describes deliverables more than business outcomes, and the section has no distinctive local or project proof to make it unmistakably Santiago’s. No unsupported claims should be added to address that gap.

## Cognitive Load

Moderate: 3 checklist failures. The section has one purpose; the six offers are grouped; headings and visual hierarchy support comparison; price and conditions are visible. Failures: nearly all service details are expanded at once; visitors still face six choices; there is no progressive disclosure for lower-priority details. The new grouping reduces comparison effort but does not shorten the mobile reading path.

## Emotional Journey

The featured Landing Page and its USD 200 starting price create a clear entry point. Explicit boundaries for catalog, hosting, and custom features build trust. The journey becomes more demanding as the visitor reads the quote-only options and terms. WhatsApp is a direct ending, but it does not say what happens after the first message or what information to prepare.

## Strengths

- Landing Page combines its starting price, scope, inclusions, and WhatsApp CTA in one prominent offer.
- Catalog exclusions, hosting/domain terms, maintenance separation, and custom-feature feasibility are stated clearly.
- Grouped service rows connect descriptions, inclusions, budget labels, and CTAs while preserving the portfolio’s palette.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The section remains a long decision surface.** Six expanded offers and four general conditions make mobile visitors read a substantial amount before deciding. Consider shortening repeated detail or revealing secondary inclusions only when needed. Suggested: `$impeccable distill`.
- **[P2] The WhatsApp handoff gives no next-step reassurance.** Visitors are not told what to include in the first message or what the initial exchange is for. Add expectation-setting only after Santiago confirms the exact promise. Suggested: `$impeccable clarify`.
